# Skill: AI Marketing Engineering

Wraps existing Caro skill: `.claude/skills/ai-marketing-engineering/`

## Purpose

AI-powered marketing engineering based on Alon Huri's framework. Transforms marketing operations through data-driven content strategy, automated campaigns, and intelligent audience targeting.

## When to Use

- Developing content marketing strategy
- Creating campaign plans for releases
- Analyzing marketing metrics and ROI
- Product positioning and messaging

## Workflow

1. **Analyze**: Review current metrics, audience data, competitive landscape
2. **Strategy**: Define content themes, channels, and frequency
3. **Create**: Draft content pieces aligned with strategy
4. **Distribute**: Schedule across channels (social, blog, email)
5. **Measure**: Track KPIs and optimize
